How It Works (Simple Explanation)

This simulator models India's electricity system with a few core relationships:

1. Demand Calculation: Starting from India's current electricity demand of ~1,500 TWh, we apply the user's demand growth rate to project future demand.

2. Energy Split: The selected renewable percentage determines how much of total demand is met by solar, wind, hydro, and other renewables. The remainder must come from coal (our model assumes coal fills the gap).

3. Emissions: Only coal produces carbon emissions in this model. We multiply coal generation by India's grid emission factor (0.82 kg CO2 per kWh).

4. Supply Check: We compare projected demand against estimated supply capacity to identify potential deficits.
