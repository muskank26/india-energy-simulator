How It Works

This tool models the relationship between renewable energy deployment, electricity demand, and carbon emissions using simplified but realistic calculations:

1. Demand Projection: Starting from a baseline demand, we apply the demand growth rate to project future electricity needs.

2. Energy Split: The renewable percentage determines how much demand is met by clean sources. The remainder is assumed to come from coal.

3. Emissions Calculation: Carbon emissions are calculated from coal generation using India's grid emission factor.

4. Supply Check: We compare projected demand against estimated generation capacity to identify potential gaps.
